Spot–futures arbitrage (Exercise 5)

Same method as the paper. Assume a spot market exists in every round, you can borrow and lend at rate r (continuous compounding), and the asset pays no income. The futures price F is the expected delivery price at the start of the round. Round t is T − t periods from delivery (T = 6), so the no-arbitrage spot price is S* = F·e−r(T−t). At delivery T − t = 0, so S* = F = the delivery price. That is spot–futures convergence.
